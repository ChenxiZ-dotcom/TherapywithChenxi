Featured workshop

Running on Empty: a brain-based approach to burnout and sleep

An interactive workshop grounded in neuroscience and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT). Participants learn how chronic stress shows up in the body as tension, pain, gut trouble and poor sleep. Then they practice tools to recover energy and keep performing without burning out.

It doesn't tell high achievers to "just relax." For people who work hard at everything, relaxing becomes one more task to get right. This workshop teaches them to work with stress instead of fighting it.

Who it's for

  • Teams in high-pressure, deadline-driven roles, such as healthcare, law, finance, tech and startups
  • Managers who want to support well-being without lowering the bar
  • HR and wellness leads planning employee well-being programs
  • Groups showing early signs of burnout: tiredness, more sick days, short tempers or lower engagement

What participants walk away with

Tools they can use the same day, not just information

01

A personal early-warning map

Each person names their own body signals of overload, such as neck tension, stomach trouble or 3 a.m. wake-ups, so they can act before it becomes a crisis.

02

The stress-body loop, explained

How the brain's alarm system turns pressure into pain, gut symptoms and poor sleep, and why pushing through often makes it louder.

03

Three sleep tools to try tonight

Practical ways to wind down a busy mind, including what to do when "trying to sleep" keeps them awake.

04

A 2-minute reset for high-stakes moments

A skill to use before a big meeting or deadline, to make room for stress and still perform.

05

One small change they commit to

A specific step they'll take this week, chosen around what matters most to them.
